## Recent Activities

**Presentation at BeGEO 2026 (Brussels)**
*Operational Flood and Emergency Mapping from Space: How Belgium, ESA and EU Copernicus Turn Data into Decisions* — presented at the [BeGEO 2026 conference](https://ngi.be/begeo/2026/track/veerkracht-met-open-data-geo-voor-wereldwijde-doelen/), showing how multi-source satellite data are processed into rapid maps for emergency services and policymakers. Slides available on request via [Contact](/contact).

**Teaching in Brazil (February 2026)**
Short course at the [Serrapilheira Institute](https://serrapilheira.org/) on Sentinel-1/2 data processing, time series analysis, and ecosystem resilience — training nationally selected researchers.

**PhD jury participation (Sion, Switzerland, 2026)**
Internal defence committee member for a PhD on geospatial methods.

**Belgian EO user engagement (2024–ongoing)**
Continued engagement with Belgian and European communities on Copernicus services, data infrastructures, and operational uptake.
